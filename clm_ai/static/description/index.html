NextContract AI

AI-assisted field extraction and clause analysis

  • Extract contract fields as suggestions that a reviewer accepts or rejects
  • Compare inbound clauses with your clause library and flag deviations and risk
  • Background AI jobs with a review queue

External service

AI processing uses Google Gemini and needs your own Gemini API key, billed by Google. Contract text and files are sent to Google only after an administrator enters the key and gives cloud-processing consent in Settings. Consent can be revoked there.

This module is part of NextContract, contract lifecycle management for Odoo 19 Enterprise. Install the NextContract app to get the complete suite.
