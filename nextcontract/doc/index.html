NextContract

NextContract is contract lifecycle management for Odoo 19 Enterprise. It takes every contract from the first request through legal review, drafting, negotiation, signature and activation, and then keeps track of it until it is renewed, terminated or archived. Everything happens inside Odoo, on the same contacts, documents, signatures and users you already have.

What NextContract does for you

The problem

How NextContract solves it

Requests arrive by email and chat, with missing details

One intake form for the business. Legal screens each request and turns it into a contract in one click.

Every contract is written from scratch, or from an old copy

A library of approved templates and clauses. Drafts are generated in Word or PDF with the contract's data already filled in, and the Word file can be edited inside Odoo.

Nobody knows who has to approve what

Approval rules create review and approval tasks for the right people, and a contract moves on only when they are done.

Negotiation happens in email attachments

The counterparty uses the Odoo portal to read, download and upload revisions. Every round is recorded, and versions are compared as a redline.

Signed contracts disappear into folders

Signing with Odoo Sign (or a paper signature), then obligations, risk and key-date alerts on every live contract, with renewals and amendments handled in the same place.

Reading every contract takes hours

The optional AI assistant reads a contract, proposes its key data and flags clauses that differ from your standard wording. It works with Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or a model you host yourself (Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio), with your own key. People review and confirm every suggestion (The AI assistant).

With NextContract you can:

  • collect contract requests from the business and screen them in Legal;

  • keep approved templates, clauses and Word document templates in one library, and edit those Word templates inside Odoo;

  • generate drafts in Word or PDF with the contract's data filled in;

  • route reviews and approvals as tasks;

  • negotiate with the counterparty through the Odoo portal, and compare versions;

  • sign with Odoo Sign, or record a signature made on paper;

  • track obligations, risk and key dates on live contracts, and renew, amend, terminate or archive them;

  • report on all contracts and export them to Excel;

  • optionally, let the AI assistant extract contract data and compare clauses with your library, using the AI provider you choose: Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or an OpenAI-compatible server on your own network.

Quick start

The first hour with NextContract, in order. Each step links to the details.

  1. Install NextContract, its Python packages and LibreOffice (Installation).

  2. Give people a role: Legal, approvers, requesters and read-only users (User roles).

  3. Check the defaults: the contract types and the two approval rules are ready to use. Change them only if your process differs (Contract types and fields, Approval rules).

  4. Publish your first template: upload your Word contract and add placeholders (Step 3: Prepare the template).

  5. Run one contract end to end, from request to activation (Your first contract, step by step).

  6. Optional: connect an AI provider so the assistant can read contracts for you. It takes about five minutes: switch AI on, add a provider with your key, test the connection (AI features (optional), then The AI assistant).

Tip

Run your first contract with a colleague playing the requester and another the approver. That way every person sees their own part of the process.

Installation

  1. Copy every folder of the NextContract package into a directory on your Odoo addons path. The Word editor is included in the nextcontract module; no other module is needed.

  2. Install the Python packages:

    pip install -r requirements.txt

  3. Install LibreOffice on the Odoo server (Debian/Ubuntu):

    apt-get install -y libreoffice-writer-nogui fonts-liberation fonts-dejavu-core

  4. Restart Odoo.

  5. Go to Apps, remove the Apps filter, search for NextContract and click Activate.

Result: the NextContract app appears on the Odoo home screen. Installing it installs the whole suite.

Important

Behind a reverse proxy (nginx, Apache, a load balancer), the Word editor needs:

  • .wasm files served with the type application/wasm;

  • same-origin framing allowed: X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN, not DENY;

  • if you set a Content-Security-Policy, 'wasm-unsafe-eval' allowed for scripts;

  • HTTPS. The copy button in the editor's Tags panel only works over HTTPS.

Contract types and fields

NextContract ‣ Configuration ‣ Contract Types already lists the common types: NDA, Services Agreement, Supply Agreement, Software/SaaS Agreement, Lease Agreement and more. Add your own as needed.

NextContract ‣ Configuration ‣ Contract Fields adds your own fields to contracts, such as Governing Law, Limitation of Liability or Payment Milestones. A field can apply to all contract types or only to some. The values are filled in on the contract's Additional Fields tab, and each field's code can be used as a placeholder in your templates.

Approval rules

NextContract ‣ Configuration ‣ Workflow Rules decides which approval tasks are created, and when. Two rules come ready to use:

  • Legal Review Approval, created when a contract is submitted for review;

  • Management Approval per DOP, created when the legal review is complete.

You can limit a rule to a contract type or a minimum value, make it optional, or assign it to the Legal Admin. A contract moves on only when the required tasks of its current step are done.

Activation checklist

NextContract ‣ Configuration ‣ Activation Checklist lists what must be filled in before a contract can go live: counterparty, type, owners, currency, dates, notice period, confidentiality, risk rating, the signed copy and the executed version. Switch off what your process does not need.

Notifications and alerts

NextContract ‣ Configuration ‣ Notifications decides who is told about which contract events. When a contract goes live, alerts are created for its key dates: expiry, renewal, notice period and others. They run on a schedule. NextContract ‣ Configuration ‣ Process Contract Alerts runs them immediately.

Step 1: Switch on AI and give consent

Who: Legal Admin, or any administrator.

  1. Go to Settings ‣ NextContract.

  2. Tick Enable AI Features. The AI Providers setting appears.

  3. If you will use a provider hosted by a third party (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, or any cloud service), click Acknowledge under Cloud Processing Consent. Odoo records who consented and when, for this company. Revoke withdraws consent at any time.

  4. Optionally, adjust the review thresholds and document limits, described below.

  5. Click Save.

Warning

Make sure your organisation allows contract content to be processed by the provider before you acknowledge. Consent covers every third-party provider of the company.

Step 2: Add a provider

Who: Legal Admin, or any administrator.

  1. Go to NextContract ‣ Configuration ‣ AI Providers (or click Manage AI Providers in Settings) and click New.

  2. Give it a Name people will recognise, for example OpenAI (Legal team), and choose its Type.

  3. Paste the API Key. Leave Base URL empty to use the vendor's standard address.

  4. Save, then click Fetch Models. NextContract asks the provider which models your key can use and lists them on the Models tab. You can also type a model ID by hand, exactly as the vendor writes it.

  5. Choose a Default Model, and optionally a Fallback Model.

  6. Click Test Connection. A green message confirms the provider answered, and which model replied. The test sends one short sentence and no contract content, so it works before consent is given.

Field

What it does

Type

Which kind of API the provider speaks: Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or Custom (OpenAI-compatible).

API Key

Your key. Stored on the provider and visible only to Legal Admins and administrators. Not needed for a local server without authentication.

Base URL

The API address. Empty means the vendor's standard address. Required for Custom, for example http://ollama:11434/v1.

Default Model

Pre-selected when someone runs an analysis with this provider. They can pick another model of the list.

Fallback Model

Tried once, automatically, when the chosen model is still overloaded after its retries. Choose a different model, one that is unlikely to be busy at the same time. The run records which model actually answered.

Structured Output

OpenAI and Custom only. How the model is asked to answer in a fixed format. Keep JSON Schema; choose JSON object or Prompt only if a self-hosted server rejects it.

Reads Images

Untick for a text-only model. Such a model cannot read scanned documents that have no text layer.

Hosted by a third party

Custom only. Untick only for a server you operate on your own network (see below).

Max Output Tokens

Anthropic only. The maximum length of the reply. Raise it if long extractions come back cut off.

Models

The models people can choose. Switch a model off to hide it without deleting it.

Result: the provider can be chosen in Extract via AI and in migration imports.

Keep contracts on your own network

To analyse contracts without sending them to an outside company, run an open model on your own server with Ollama, vLLM or LM Studio, and connect it as a Custom provider:

  1. Create a provider with Type Custom (OpenAI-compatible).

  2. Enter the server's Base URL as the Odoo server sees it, ending in /v1, for example http://ollama.internal:11434/v1. With Docker, localhost means the Odoo container itself, so use the server's host name.

  3. Leave API Key empty unless your server asks for one.

  4. Untick Hosted by a third party. The blue consent note disappears: content stays on your network, so no consent is needed for this provider.

  5. Add the model ID exactly as the server names it, for example llama3.1:70b, or click Fetch Models.

  6. Click Test Connection.

Tip

Local models vary much more than hosted ones. Use a large, recent model, and a vision model if you analyse scans. If the server rejects requests or the answers cannot be read, set Structured Output to JSON object.

Note

Only untick Hosted by a third party for a server you operate. A hosted service such as OpenRouter, or a gateway run by someone else, is a third party: leave it ticked so consent is enforced.

Choose which contract fields the AI fills in

The AI always looks for the contract's title, dates, value, currency, counterparty and contract type. It can also fill the extra fields you defined in NextContract ‣ Configuration ‣ Contract Fields:

  1. Open a field and keep AI Extractable ticked.

  2. Under AI Extraction Hint, tell the AI exactly what to take and what to ignore, for example The notice period for termination for convenience, in days. Ignore the cure period in the breach clause.

A clear hint is the best way to improve the AI's answers for your contracts.

Your first contract, step by step

A contract moves through these statuses. The status bar at the top of every contract shows where it is, and the buttons next to it show what can be done now.

Draft Request → Under Legal Review → Internal Review Completed → Under Negotiation → Approved for Signature → Pending Signature → Executed → Active

Step 1: Request a contract

Who: anyone with a NextContract role who needs a contract.

  1. Go to NextContract ‣ Intake ‣ Contract Requests and click New.

  2. Fill in what you know: title, counterparty (and who signs for them), contract type, department, purpose, estimated value, start date and urgency. Attach any supporting documents.

  3. Click Submit Request.

Result: the request gets a reference such as REQ-2026-00144, and Legal sees it in the list of requests.

Step 2: Screen the request (Legal)

Who: Legal Reviewer or Legal Admin.

  1. Open the request and click Start Legal Intake.

  2. Check the details and documents, then click Accept Request, or Reject Request with a reason.

  3. On an accepted request, click Convert to Contract.

Result: a contract is created with the request's details, with a reference such as CNT-2026-00771. You can also create contracts directly in NextContract ‣ Contracts ‣ All Contracts.

Step 3: Prepare the template

Who: Legal Admin. Do this once per type of contract; every contract of that type then reuses it.

Templates and clauses live in NextContract ‣ Library. Clauses hold your standard wording. A template combines a contract type, its clauses and a Document Template: the Word file used to produce contracts.

  1. Go to NextContract ‣ Library ‣ Templates, click New, and give the template a name and contract type.

  2. On the Document Template tab, click Upload and choose your .docx contract. The first file is kept as the original and is never overwritten.

  3. Put placeholders where the contract's data should appear, for example {{ partner_name }} or {{ effective_date }}. The Placeholders panel lists them all (see Placeholder reference).

  4. Click Insert Clause Collection to add the template's clauses, and Insert signature block for a signature section for both parties.

  5. Click Validate, then Save Changes, or Save as New Template Version.

  6. Click Submit for Review, then Approve, then Publish.

To edit the Word file without leaving Odoo, click Word Editor on the Document Template tab.

  • Save (or Ctrl+S) saves onto the active version. If that version is the original, or a contract already uses it, a new version is created instead and you are told.

  • Save as New Version always creates a new version and makes it active.

  • Check Tags checks the placeholders without saving.

  • If someone else saved the template after you opened it, your save is refused. Nothing is overwritten. Use Download my copy to keep your edits, or Reload latest.

Result: the template is Published and can be chosen on contracts. Contracts already drafted keep the version they were drafted with.

Step 4: Draft the contract

Who: Legal.

  1. Open the contract and choose a published Contract Template.

  2. Click Generate Draft and choose Word (DOCX), PDF or a plain text preview.

  3. Change the wording for this contract with Edit Contract. Keep a new version with Create New Version.

  4. Preview Contract, Generate DOCX and Generate PDF produce the current version at any time.

Result: the draft is stored on the Document Versions tab, and in NextContract ‣ Contracts ‣ Contract Versions. Each version has a type (Internal Draft, Counterparty Draft, Signature Draft and so on). Activate Version makes a version the working one.

Step 5: Review and approve

Who: Legal, the approvers, and Finance, Procurement or Compliance when Legal asks for their input.

  1. On the contract, click Submit for Review. The Legal Review Approval task appears on the Workflow Tasks tab.

  2. If needed, ask other teams with Request Business Input, Request Finance Input, Request Procurement Input or Request Compliance Review.

  3. When the legal review is done, click Complete Internal Review. This creates the management approval task.

Approvers do not need to search for contracts. Their tasks wait in NextContract ‣ Workflow ‣ My Tasks, where they click Approve, Reject or Delegate.

Result: when the management approval is given, the contract is Approved for Signature. To send it back, use Return for Clarification; to stop it, Cancel Contract.

Step 6: Negotiate with the counterparty

Who: Legal. The counterparty uses the Odoo portal; they need no Odoo licence.

Important

Give the counterparty's contact portal access first. In Contacts, open the contact, then use Action ‣ Grant portal access. A counterparty who is given portal access only after the contract is sent does not see the contract.

  1. On the contract (or on one of its draft versions), click Send to Counterparty. Check the version and the counterparty, add a response deadline and a note, then click Send for Negotiation.

  2. The counterparty signs in to the portal and opens Your Contracts. They can read and download the shared version, and upload a revised document with comments.

  3. Each exchange is a negotiation round in NextContract ‣ Negotiation ‣ Negotiation Rounds.

  4. On a version, Compare Versions or Compare with previous shows a redline of the changes. Comparisons are kept in NextContract ‣ Negotiation ‣ Version Comparisons.

Result: the round is recorded with its deadline, and each revision is kept as a contract version.

Step 7: Sign

Who: Legal sends; the signers sign with Odoo Sign.

  1. Click Prepare Signature Draft. The final version becomes the Signature Draft. It must be active and have a document attached.

  2. Click Send for Signature. Every signer needs a valid email address.

  3. Follow progress on the Signature & Execution tab.

If the contract was signed on paper or outside Odoo, click Mark as Manually Signed instead. Upload the signed PDF, enter the signing date and signatory, and confirm that the document is fully executed.

Result: when everyone has signed, the contract is Executed. The Final Contract tab has the signed contract, with Download PDF and Download DOCX.

Step 8: Activate

Who: Legal.

  1. Open the Activation Checklist tab and fill in anything still missing, usually the effective, expiry and signing dates.

  2. Click Activate Contract.

Result: the contract is Active, and alerts for its key dates are created.

Step 9: Manage the live contract

Who: Legal, Finance and the contract's owners.

  • Obligations: payments, deliverables, SLAs, notices and renewals, each with an owner and a due date. Mark them Complete when done; overdue ones raise alerts.

  • Risk Assessment: add risk factors and click Recalculate Risk to update the score and rating.

  • Alerts: expiry, renewal, notice period, bank guarantee, insurance and other dates.

  • Create Amendment starts a change to a signed contract. It goes through review and signature again; the signed version stays locked.

  • Renew creates the renewed contract (recommended) or renews this one in place, with new dates and terms.

  • Terminate records the date and reason and stops the alerts. Mark Expired and Archive close the contract.

Click View Lifecycle on any contract to see its whole journey: which steps are done, skipped, current or to come, who it is waiting on, and the overall progress.

The AI assistant

What it is for

A contract is long, and the facts that matter are scattered through it: who the parties are, when it starts and ends, what it is worth, and whether the wording is the wording you agreed to use. Finding them means reading every page and typing the answers into the system.

The AI assistant does the first reading for you. It reads a contract document and gives you two kinds of suggestion:

Analysis

What the AI does

What you get

Field extraction

Reads the document and finds the contract's key data.

Suggested values for the title, effective and expiry dates, contract value, currency, counterparty and contract type, and for any of your own contract fields. Each comes with a confidence score, the page it was found on and the sentence it was taken from.

Clause analysis

Compares each clause in the document with the standard wording in your clause library.

A classification (Standard, Approved Alternative, Modified, Non-Standard or Rejected), a risk level from Low to Critical, and a plain-language explanation of what is different.

Full analysis

Both of the above in one run.

Everything above.

It saves the most time on:

  • drafts sent by the counterparty, where you need to know quickly what differs from your standard terms;

  • contracts that already exist and have to be entered into NextContract, one by one or as an import (Import existing contracts);

  • any signed document whose data nobody has typed in.

The AI suggests, people decide

AI can misread a document, so NextContract never lets it change a contract on its own.

  • Nothing is written until a Legal Reviewer confirms. Suggestions are held in a review queue, and the contract stays as it was until then.

  • Every suggestion shows its evidence: the page and the exact text it came from, so a reviewer can check it in seconds.

  • Doubtful answers are not hidden. Low-confidence suggestions block the confirmation until someone has looked at them.

  • The AI can only suggest values for a short list of fields: title, dates, value, currency, counterparty, contract type and your own contract fields. It cannot change approvals, signatures or statuses.

  • It is traceable. Each run records who started it, which provider and model read the document, who gave consent, who reviewed it and when, and how many tokens it used.

  • It is off until you switch it on. No contract content leaves Odoo until an administrator has connected a provider and, for a third-party provider, given consent (AI features (optional)). With a provider on your own network, nothing leaves it at all.

Step 1: Start an analysis

Who: Legal Reviewer or Legal Admin.

  1. Open the contract, go to the Document Versions tab and open the version to analyse. The version needs a document; upload or generate one first.

  2. Click Extract via AI.

  1. Choose the Analysis Type: Field Extraction, Clause Analysis or Full Analysis (Fields + Clauses).

  2. Choose the AI Provider. The first provider of the list is proposed.

  3. Check the Model. It starts with the provider's default model; pick another one of the provider's models if you need to.

  4. Click Run AI Analysis. The analysis job opens, with the status Ready.

  1. Check the job, then click Run Analysis.

Result: the AI reads the document while you wait. It takes from a few seconds to a few minutes, depending on the length of the document and on the model. The job then shows Completed, and its suggestions wait in the review queue. If the model is overloaded and a Fallback Model is set on the provider, the fallback is tried once, automatically.

Step 2: Follow the analysis

Every run is listed in two places:

  • on the version, in the AI Analysis Jobs tab;

  • for reviewers, in NextContract ‣ Extraction Reviews, the queue of all runs.

A job you start yourself goes from Ready to Processing, then to Completed or Failed. Jobs started in bulk, by Run New Extraction or by a migration import, are Pending first: a background worker picks them up, usually within a few minutes, so you can keep working.

When a job fails, the reason is shown on the job, for example missing consent, a document that is too long or unreadable, or an error from the provider (see Troubleshooting). Fix the cause, then click Retry Analysis on the job.

Step 3: Review the suggested field values

Who: Legal Reviewer or Legal Admin.

Open a completed run in NextContract ‣ Extraction Reviews. The top of the page shows which provider and model read the document, how many pages it read, and the consent under which the document was sent. A banner says whether the run is Ready to confirm, or what still needs attention.

The suggestions are sorted into three tabs by confidence, using the thresholds set in AI features (optional):

Tab

Confidence

What to do

Needs Attention

Below the low threshold, or a value that must be matched to an existing record

Check each one. Open it to read the source text, then click Approve if it is right, or Reject if it is not. When a value such as a counterparty or contract type must be matched to a record in Odoo, click Resolve (or Map / Resolve) and choose the right record. The review cannot be confirmed until this tab is empty.

Review

Between the two thresholds

Probably right, but worth a look. Correct the suggested value in place if needed.

Verified

Very likely right. They are shown in full, with their source text, so you can still check them.

Three more tabs help you understand a run: Source Document shows the text the AI read, Field Configuration Used shows which fields were requested and with which hints, and Cost shows the input and output tokens the provider reported. Your provider's billing page shows the actual charge.

Note

A contract field marked as required must have a value, either from the AI or already on the contract. If it has neither, the banner tells you how many required fields are missing.

Step 4: Confirm and apply

When the banner turns green (Ready to confirm):

  1. Click Confirm & Apply. Every suggestion that is still pending is written to the contract, and the run is marked Reviewed with your name and the date.

  2. Or click Reject Extraction to discard the whole run. The contract is not changed.

Result: the contract's fields are filled in, and the run stays in the queue as a record of what was suggested and who accepted it. To try again, for example after improving a field's hint, click Run New Extraction. It uses the same provider and model, and runs in the background. Suggestions of the earlier run that nobody decided on are replaced by the new ones.

Tip

Reject any single value you do not want before you click Confirm & Apply: everything still pending is applied.

Step 5: Review clause deviations

Who: Legal Reviewer or Legal Admin.

For Clause Analysis and Full Analysis, open the run from the version's AI Analysis Jobs tab and go to the Clause Deviations tab. It lists every clause the AI compared with your library, with its classification and risk.

Click Review on a line to open it:

  • Text Comparison puts your Library Standard Wording next to the Wording in This Version.

  • Classification & Risk shows the AI's classification, risk level, confidence and explanation, which you cannot edit, next to the Legal Confirmation, which you fill in.

Click Confirm Classification to accept the AI's view, or change the classification, risk level and rationale first. The confirmed values are the authoritative ones; the AI's suggestion is kept for reference.

Result: deviations you confirmed as High or Critical are added as risk factors on the contract's Risk Assessment tab the next time you click Recalculate Risk, if they belong to the contract's active version.

Getting the best results

  • Use the original file. A Word file or a PDF with selectable text is read most accurately. Scans work, but the quality of the scan matters.

  • Write good hints. For each contract field, an AI Extraction Hint that says what to take and what to ignore makes the biggest difference (Choose which contract fields the AI fills in).

  • Keep your clause library current. Clause analysis compares with the standard wording of your published clauses (up to 50). With no published clause, it has nothing to compare with and is skipped.

  • Match the analysis to the need. Use Field Extraction for data entry and Clause Analysis for incoming drafts. Full Analysis reads the document once for both.

  • Match the model to the work. A stronger model pays off on clause analysis of complex agreements; a faster, cheaper one is often enough for field extraction and bulk imports. Create one provider per need, or keep several models on one provider and choose per run.

  • Set a fallback model. Busy periods at a provider then cost you a short delay instead of a failed run.

  • Keep an eye on cost. The Cost tab of each run shows the tokens used. Long documents, and the optional searchable text layer, use more.

Import existing contracts

Legal Admins can bring existing contracts in through NextContract ‣ Configuration ‣ Migration ‣ Migration Batches.

  1. Prepare a ZIP file with contracts.csv (or contracts.xlsx) and the contract PDFs. The columns are: Vendor Name, Contract Title, Contract Number, Effective Date, Expiry Date, Execution Date, Contract Value, Legacy Source Reference and Document Path (the PDF's path inside the ZIP).

  2. Create a batch, upload the ZIP and click Parse File.

  3. Click Run Deduplication & Validation and check the Staging Records.

  4. Click Import Valid Records.

  5. Optionally, click Run AI Extraction to fill in missing data from the PDFs. Choose which contracts to process (all of the batch, or only those missing a value or a date), the AI Provider and the Model. The runs are queued and processed in the background; review them in NextContract ‣ Extraction Reviews.

The ZIP may be up to 200 MB, or 1 GB once unpacked, with at most 2,000 files. Only PDF documents are accepted.

Frequently asked questions

Can we use the Word contracts we already have?

Yes. Upload each one as a template's Document Template and replace the variable parts with placeholders.

What happens to signed or drafted contracts when a template changes?

Nothing. A contract keeps the template version it was drafted with. Only new contracts use the new version.

Can the counterparty change our document?

They upload a revised version through the portal. You compare it with yours and decide what to accept.

Do we have to use Odoo Sign?

No. The Sign app must be installed, but you can sign on paper and use Mark as Manually Signed.

Who can see contract values?

Legal, Finance and Approvers. Requesters, Procurement and read-only users do not.

How are we reminded about renewals?

When a contract goes live, alerts are created for its expiry, renewal and notice dates. You can also add your own obligations with due dates.

Can we change the approval steps?

Yes, in NextContract ‣ Configuration ‣ Workflow Rules (see Approval rules).

Is the AI required?

No. Everything works without it. Nothing is sent to an AI provider unless an administrator has connected one and, for a third-party provider, given consent.

Which AI providers can we use?

Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, and any server that offers an OpenAI-compatible API: Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, OpenRouter or your own AI gateway. You can connect several and choose one for each run.

Can we keep contract data on our own network?

Yes. Run an open model on your own server and connect it as a Custom provider with Hosted by a third party unticked (see Keep contracts on your own network). The contract then never leaves your infrastructure.

Can we try the AI without an API key?

Yes, to see the review screens. In Extract via AI, clear the AI Provider field and choose Mock (Testing) as Built-in Provider. It returns sample suggestions and sends nothing anywhere. The values are invented, so reject the run afterwards.

Will the AI change my contracts by itself?

Never. It only makes suggestions. A Legal Reviewer checks them and clicks Confirm & Apply before anything is written to a contract.

How do I know if I can trust a suggestion?

Each one shows a confidence score, the page and the sentence it was taken from. Low-confidence values must be checked before the review can be confirmed.

What does the AI cost?

NextContract adds no charge. Your provider bills the use of your API key; a server you host yourself costs only its hardware. The Cost tab of each run shows the tokens used.

What is sent to the AI provider?

The document of the version you analyse (or its text) and the list of fields to find. For clause analysis, also the standard wording of your published clauses. To a third-party provider, it is sent only after consent has been recorded for the company. Test Connection sends one short test sentence and no contract content.

Can the AI read scanned contracts?

Yes, but results depend on the quality of the scan. Check the suggestions more carefully, and use the Source Document tab to see what the AI read.

Upgrading

  1. Back up the database and the filestore.

  2. Download the new version from your Odoo Apps orders page.

  3. Replace the NextContract folders with the new ones. Replace whole folders; do not merge them.

  4. If the release notes mention new requirements, run pip install -r requirements.txt.

  5. Restart Odoo and upgrade the modules:

    odoo -c <your config> -d <database> -u nextcontract --stop-after-init

    Then start Odoo normally. Or, in Apps with developer mode active, open NextContract and click Upgrade.

  6. Ask users to refresh their browser once (Ctrl+F5).

Upgrading NextContract also upgrades the Word editor, the AI features, purchase-order links and contract migration, which are all part of the one module.

Troubleshooting

Problem

Solution

"requires LibreOffice on the Odoo server" when previewing or exporting a PDF

Install LibreOffice (see Installation) and restart Odoo.

The Word editor stays blank or does not start

Behind a reverse proxy: serve .wasm as application/wasm, allow X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN and, with a Content-Security-Policy, 'wasm-unsafe-eval'. Use Chrome or Edge.

The Word editor looks unchanged after an upgrade

Refresh the browser with Ctrl+F5.

The Word Editor button is missing

It appears once the template has a Document Template. Upload a .docx on the Document Template tab.

"Copy failed" in the Tags panel

Copying needs HTTPS. Select the placeholder text and copy it by hand.

"Someone saved a newer Template Version"

Someone saved after you opened the editor. Use Download my copy, then Reload latest, and reapply your edits.

The counterparty sees no contract in the portal

Their contact needs portal access before the contract is sent (see Step 6: Negotiate with the counterparty). Send it again after granting access.

"Cloud AI processing is not enabled"

Acknowledge cloud processing for the company (see Step 1: Switch on AI and give consent), or use a provider on your own network.

"Gemini API Key is not configured", or "needs a configured provider"

The run has no provider. Add one in NextContract ‣ Configuration ‣ AI Providers (see Step 2: Add a provider) and choose it in Extract via AI.

Test Connection says "Connection failed"

The message is the provider's own answer. Check the API key, the Base URL (with /v1 for most OpenAI-compatible servers) and that the Odoo server can reach the address. A model ID must be written exactly as the vendor writes it; Fetch Models lists the valid ones.

"A Custom provider needs a Base URL"

Enter the server's address, starting with http:// or https://.

An AI run is Failed with the reason Provider Error

The provider did not accept the request. Check that the API key is valid and has quota, and that the model is still offered (Fetch Models, then choose another model). Set a Fallback Model for busy periods. Then click Retry Analysis.

An AI run is Failed with the reason Invalid Provider Response

The model's answer could not be read. With Anthropic, raise Max Output Tokens. With a self-hosted model, set Structured Output to JSON object, or use a larger model.

An AI run is Failed with the reason Document Unreadable

The document is a scan without text and the model cannot read images, or the file is not a PDF, Word or text document. Use a model that reads images, and keep Reads Images ticked on its provider.

An AI run is Failed with the reason Document Too Long

Split the document, or raise the page limit in Document Limits (see Other AI settings).

An AI run stays Ready

Open it and click Run Analysis. Nothing is sent until you do.

An AI run stays Pending

Bulk runs start within five minutes. If they do not, ask your administrator to check that the scheduled action Process Pending AI Jobs is active.

Confirm & Apply is greyed out

The banner says why: open the Needs Attention tab and approve, reject or map every value on it (see Step 3: Review the suggested field values).

Clause analysis lists nothing

Publish clauses in NextContract ‣ Library ‣ Clauses. Clause analysis compares with published clauses only.

"Only approved contracts can be sent for signature" or "No active Signature Draft found"

Finish the approvals, then click Prepare Signature Draft before Send for Signature.

"Complete the Activation Checklist first"

Open the Activation Checklist tab and fill in what is missing.

A user cannot see a contract

Check their NextContract role and allowed companies. Restricted contracts are visible only to Legal and to the contract's owners.

Data and privacy

  • Contracts, documents and their versions stay in your Odoo database and filestore.

  • AI features send contract text and files to the AI provider chosen for the run, with your API key. For a third-party provider (Google, OpenAI, Anthropic or any hosted service), only after an administrator has given consent for the company; consent can be revoked at any time. A provider on your own network keeps the content in-house.

  • API keys are stored in your database and visible only to Legal Admins and administrators.

  • The Word editor runs in the browser and saves to your Odoo server. Common fonts are included. Choosing a font that is not included may load it from Google Fonts, which receives the font name, not the document.

  • Counterparties see only the contract versions shared with them through the portal.
