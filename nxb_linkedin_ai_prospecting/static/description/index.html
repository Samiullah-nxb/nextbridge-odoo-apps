AI Prospecting Agents for LinkedIn

Turn LinkedIn into a steady source of qualified leads without leaving Odoo. AI agents write personal invitations, hold the first conversations and follow up — while Odoo controls pace, timing and safety and drops every interested prospect straight into your CRM pipeline.

What it does

Built for sales teams that prospect on LinkedIn every day.

🤖

A team of AI agents

Each target audience gets its own agent for invitations and its own agent for conversations. Two more agents chase silent prospects and remind your reps. Start, pause or stop any of them in one click.

👥

Your whole sales team

Connect as many LinkedIn accounts as you have reps. Every account keeps its own audiences, owner, pacing and connection method.

⚙️

Runs on your server

A private automation browser is installed from the Settings page and lives next to your Odoo data. Nothing to install by hand, no third-party cloud in between.

✍️

Personal, not templated

Every invitation and message is written for that one person, from their LinkedIn profile and the conversation so far. Your guidelines steer the tone; no two prospects get the same text.

🎯

Hand-off to sales

The moment a prospect shows real interest, a CRM lead appears with a short brief of who they are, what they need and what the rep should do next — and the AI steps aside.

🛡️

Human pace

Activity stays within the caps you set, only during the prospect's business hours, with irregular pauses between actions — and backs off automatically when LinkedIn signals a limit.

📝

Content calendar

Jot down a topic, get a ready-to-edit LinkedIn post, approve it and pick a publishing time. Engagement on published posts is collected for you.

📊

Results you can see

Acceptance and reply rates, a conversion funnel, daily activity and a map of where your prospects are — filterable by rep and audience.

🔍

Full audit trail

Requirements

  • Odoo 18 Community or Enterprise. Dependencies: CRM, Calendar, Discuss, Contacts, Sales Teams (all standard).
  • An AI assistant that supports custom remote MCP connectors (Model Context Protocol over HTTPS). No AI API key is stored in Odoo.
  • Your Odoo must be reachable over HTTPS from the internet so the AI assistant can call the connector URL.
  • Server option: Linux host (x86_64 or arm64) with outbound internet. Plan roughly 1–1.5 GB of RAM for each LinkedIn account that is actively connected, and about 1.1 GB of disk once for the automation browser.
  • Server option: some operating-system libraries for the browser; if any are missing, Settings shows the exact install command for your administrator.
  • Server option: the connected LinkedIn accounts must use LinkedIn in English.
  • Browser option: Google Chrome with an AI browser extension able to use MCP connectors.
  • Run Odoo with workers (workers and max_cron_threads) in production so scheduled actions run for every database.

Getting started in four steps

For the server option. The browser option is described right after.

1Prepare the automation browser

After installing, go to LinkedIn Sales › Configuration › Settings and click Prepare engine. The download takes a few minutes; click Refresh status until it reads Ready. If your server lacks a system library, the page shows the command to give your administrator.

2Link your AI assistant

Click New connector URL, then Generate. Copy the address — for security it is displayed only once — and register it in your AI assistant as a custom MCP connector without authentication. You can restrict a URL to selected accounts and revoke it whenever you like.

3Register each LinkedIn account

Under Agents › LinkedIn Profiles, create one record per rep: a name, their LinkedIn address, the Odoo user who owns the leads, and the connection method Built-in engine.

4Sign the account in

Click Connect LinkedIn and choose how: sign in on LinkedIn's own login page displayed inside Odoo (two-step verification supported), upload a saved browser session, or paste the session cookie. Test connection then confirms the account name.

Using the browser option instead

Ideal for Odoo.sh — LinkedIn stays in the rep's own Chrome.

  • Link your AI assistant with a connector URL exactly as in step 2.
  • Create the LinkedIn Profile with the connection method AI browser agent.
  • On the rep's computer, open a Chrome profile that is logged in to that LinkedIn account and has your AI browser extension with the Odoo connector.
  • Start the agent with the browser prompt from Settings. It first proves it is on the right LinkedIn account, then works through the tasks Odoo gives it and asks Odoo's permission before anything is sent.

Define your offer and your audiences

The agents only talk about what you sell, to the people you choose.

Services

Describe each offer in a few sentences with its main benefits, attach a portfolio file or link and choose the CRM tags for resulting leads. Contact numbers and emails are only shared if a prospect asks for them.

Personas

A persona is one target audience: the roles, regions and keywords to look for, the profiles to skip, and the problems they typically face. It also sets the prospect's time zone, business days and hours, and how long to wait between actions. Matching people are found and queued automatically.

Invitation agent

Choose how many invitations go out per day and per week, an overall target, whether a personal note is added and what it should focus on. People without a Connect button can be followed instead, and unanswered invitations can be withdrawn after a set time. Pausing or stopping never deletes anyone.

Conversation agent

Outline the conversation as a few goals — for example learn their current system, understand the pain, check timing — and mark the step where sales should take over. Add your meeting link, preferred tone and language. The AI adapts each message to the person rather than reciting a script.

Follow-up and reminder agents

Set how long to wait before nudging a prospect who went quiet and how many nudges to send. When a rep has taken over a conversation, any new reply creates a to-do for them with a due date.

Talk to your agents in plain language

Copy a ready-made instruction from Settings, or simply ask.

Settings contains instructions for a full run, sending invitations immediately, answering replies, a recurring scheduled run and the browser option. Everyday requests also work, for example:

  • "Answer today's LinkedIn replies."
  • "Send the queued invitations now."
  • "Invite linkedin.com/in/… from my account."
  • "How many invitations went out today?"
  • "I'll handle Ali Raza myself."

Guardrails built into Odoo

The AI proposes; Odoo decides what actually happens. These protections cannot be bypassed by the AI:

  • Volume caps — per day, per week and overall, for each audience.
  • Business hours only — measured in the prospect's own time zone.
  • Irregular pacing — random gaps, and never two actions at once on one account.
  • Automatic back-off — invitations pause when LinkedIn reports a weekly limit or stops accepting them, then resume on their own.
  • Invitation checks — each invitation is confirmed on LinkedIn afterwards; declined ones are never repeated without a manager's approval.
  • Last-second check — the conversation is re-read just before a message goes out; if something new arrived, the message is held back.
  • No duplicates — a text identical to one already sent to that person is rejected.
  • Human override — taking over a prospect, or writing to them yourself on LinkedIn, silences the agents for that person.
  • Do-not-contact list — respected by every audience and account.
  • No blind retries — if it is unclear whether something was sent, a person is asked to check instead.
  • Account protection — accounts that LinkedIn restricts or signs out are paused and their owner is notified.
  • Reading budget — a daily cap on pages viewed per account.

Data protection

  • Connector addresses contain a long random key that is stored only as a hash and hidden from server logs.
  • The AI connector works through its own restricted technical user.
  • LinkedIn passwords and verification codes pass straight to LinkedIn and are never saved.
  • Browser sessions are kept in a private folder on your server; each automation browser only accepts local connections.
  • All prospect data stays in your Odoo database.

FAQ

Which AI assistant do I need?
Any assistant that can add a custom MCP connector over HTTPS. Odoo never stores an AI API key — you use your own AI subscription.

Can I use it on Odoo.sh?
Yes, with the browser option. The server option needs a self-hosted Linux server. Odoo Online does not accept custom apps.

LinkedIn asks for extra verification when signing in from Odoo.
Choose Import session or Paste cookie in the connection wizard and use a session from your usual computer.

Why did invitations stop for a few days?
LinkedIn caps weekly invitations, especially on new accounts. The app pauses invitations for that account and resumes automatically; conversations with existing connections continue.

Can I read messages before they are sent?
Yes. Everything waits in the Sending queue, where you can review, edit or drop it.

How quickly are replies noticed?
By default the inbox is checked hourly. You can shorten the interval in Settings or ask the AI to answer replies immediately.
